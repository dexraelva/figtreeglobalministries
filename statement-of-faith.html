Statement of Faith

What we believe shapes how we serve.

Fig Tree Global Mission is committed to a Christ-centered, Scripture-rooted ministry that honors the church and the people of the land.

01

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God, trustworthy and authoritative for faith and life.

02

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is Lord, the Son of God, fully God and fully man, whose death and resurrection bring salvation.

03

The Gospel

We believe the Gospel is for every nation and that the church is called to proclaim it with truth, humility, and love.

04

The Church

We believe the local church is central to discipleship, fellowship, worship, prayer, and mission.

05

Prayer

We believe prayer is foundational to every journey, every ministry, and every step of obedience.

06

Service

We believe every believer is invited to serve God's purposes with humility and faithfulness.
