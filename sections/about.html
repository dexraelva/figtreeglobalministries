About Us

More than a journey

The Bible is connected to real places, real people, and real history. Fig Tree Global Ministries helps believers experience the land of the Bible through meaningful pilgrimage, missionary visits, fellowship, prayer, and ministry.

A deeper context

From Jerusalem to Galilee, Bethlehem to the Mount of Olives, the land provides geographical and historical context for Scripture.

A renewed burden

We encourage believers to see beyond the sights, pray with greater understanding, and return home burdened for God's work.
