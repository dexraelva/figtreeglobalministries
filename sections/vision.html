Our Vision

To see believers go where the Bible happened

We envision Christians from different nations prayerfully journeying to Israel, experiencing the biblical land, connecting with fellow believers, and developing a deeper appreciation for God's Word and His work.

Our Mission

Encouraging, equipping, and connecting

Encourage

Help believers and missionaries prayerfully consider visiting Israel.

Equip

Prepare pilgrims spiritually and practically for a purposeful journey.

Connect

Connect churches, ministries, missionaries, and believers.

Pray

Pray for Jerusalem, Israel, the people of the land, and the Gospel.

Inspire

Send visitors home with a deeper burden for missions.
