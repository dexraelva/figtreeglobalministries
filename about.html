About Fig Tree

A ministry shaped by Scripture and the land.

We exist to encourage churches, missionaries, pastors, and believers to experience Israel in a way that deepens faith and strengthens the church's heart for missions.

Our story

More than a trip. A spiritual pilgrimage.

Fig Tree Global Mission was born from a burden to help people connect the pages of Scripture with the places where biblical history unfolded.

We believe a thoughtful visit to Israel can create space for prayer, learning, fellowship, and renewed obedience. Our work is centered on serving the local church and encouraging those already carrying the Gospel to the nations.
