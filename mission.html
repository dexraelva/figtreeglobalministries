Our Mission

Encouraging the church to go, see, pray, and serve.

We help churches and missionaries experience Israel with biblical purpose, spiritual preparation, and a heart turned toward God's mission in the world.

01

Encourage meaningful pilgrimage

Make space for believers to encounter the biblical land beyond ordinary tourism.

02

Equip churches and missionaries

Offer practical guidance and spiritual preparation for groups and ministry workers.

03

Connect faith with missions

Build relationships that strengthen prayer, fellowship, and a burden for the nations.

04

Send people home renewed

Help each traveler return with a deeper love for Scripture and readiness to serve.

Our posture

We travel with open Bibles and open hearts.

“The land is not the destination of our faith. It is a place that can renew the direction of our faith.”
