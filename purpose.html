Our Purpose

To turn a visit into a deeper walk.

We create purposeful opportunities for people to see the biblical land, listen to God's Word, pray for the region, and return ready to encourage others.

Our commitments

Five ways we serve the journey.

1

Biblical context

We help travelers connect place, history, and Scripture.

2

Spiritual preparation

We encourage prayerful preparation before anyone boards a plane.

3

Church partnership

We work alongside churches rather than apart from them.

4

Missionary encouragement

We make room for ministry workers to be refreshed and strengthened.

5

Faithful return

We ask how each journey can shape life and service back home.
