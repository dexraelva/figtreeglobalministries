About Us

More than a journey

The Bible is not only a collection of stories — it is connected to real places, real people, and real history. Israel holds a unique place in the story of Scripture. From Jerusalem to Galilee, from Bethlehem to the Mount of Olives, the land provides a deeper geographical and historical context for the Word of God.

Fig Tree Global Ministries exists to encourage believers to experience the land of the Bible through meaningful pilgrimage, missionary visits, fellowship, prayer, and ministry — to see beyond the sights, to pray with greater understanding, and to return with a renewed burden for God's work.

Don't just read the Bible. Walk through its history.

Our Vision

To see believers go where the Bible happened

Our vision is to see Christians from different nations prayerfully journey to Israel, experience the biblical land, connect with fellow believers, and develop a deeper appreciation for God's Word and His work.

Our Story

How the ministry began

Every ministry begins with a burden. Fig Tree Global Ministries was born from a desire to encourage believers to develop a deeper connection with the biblical land and a greater burden for missions, prayer, and the Word of God.

The name "Fig Tree" carries biblical significance and reminds us of the connection between the land, Scripture, Israel, and God's unfolding story. What began as a burden to encourage people to visit Israel has grown into a vision to connect believers and missionaries around a shared desire: to go, to see, to pray, to learn, to serve.
